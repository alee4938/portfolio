WholeEarthOS

Web / Interactive

Open in a new tab

"We are as gods and might as well get good at it." - Whole Earth Catalogue

The Whole Earth Catalogue was an American countercultural magazine that ran from 1968 to 1998. Looking at an issue from 1988, the same year the Macintosh SE was released, I was captivated by the ideas of future technology from the past. I made this webpage as a recreation of the original Macintosh desktop. Clicking the floppy disk icon opens random windows with images from this magazine's issue, and they can be dragged and interacted with. The window titles are randomly selected from a list of the issue's tech-related terms.

Made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
